\begin{theorem}[Design $\to$ goal~(iii) --- schematic]
\label{thm:chain}
Fix classical level $\alpha$ and a family $C^{(\alpha)}$ satisfying joint fixed-$\theta$
validity (FV) on a valid support $\Theta_{\mathrm{val}}$
(Part~II Ass.~FV; for score-threshold sets, $\Theta_{\mathrm{val}}=\{\theta:F(\theta)\ge 1-\alpha\}$).
Let $A_r$ be the noisy-objective (or soft-argmin) map of
\texttt{part\_i\_randomized\_design.tex} with user budget $\eta_r\to 0$,
Laplace scale $b_r=2\varepsilon/\eta_r$ (or $\beta_r=\eta_r/(2\varepsilon)$),
\textbf{restricted so that $A_r$ and its oracle $A_0$ are supported on $\Theta_{\mathrm{val}}$}
(nested: net of $[t_\alpha,1]$; tradeoff: conservative $\Theta_0^\varepsilon\subseteq\{g^\star\le 0\}$),
and concentration $(\varepsilon(n,\nu_r),\nu_r)$ from
\texttt{instance\_nested\_uq.tex} or \texttt{instance\_tradeoff\_uq.tex}
(DKW $\varepsilon=\sqrt{\log(2/\nu)/(2n)}$), via Ass.\ concentration.
Then $A_r$ is $(\eta_r,0,\nu_r)$-stable and~(OV) holds at $q=\alpha$.
If the fixed-$\theta$ map of $C^{(\alpha)}$
is unchanged by the noise in $A_r$, Part~II Cor.\ vanish yields
\[
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{Y\notin C^{(\alpha)}(X;D,A_r(D))\bigr\}
\le e^{\eta_r}\alpha+\nu_r,
\qquad
\mathrm{Infl}_r\le (e^{\eta_r}-1)\alpha+\nu_r\to 0
\]
along any sequence with $\eta_r\to 0$ and $\nu_r\to 0$ (e.g.\ $n\to\infty$ tightening
concentration, or $\nu_r$ absorbed into a fixed Part~II error budget).
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}
Operational W5 answer to (iii): pay selection softness (large $b_r$) to
buy small $\eta_r$; keep classical level of $\mathcal{C}$ fixed; keep selection inside
$\Theta_{\mathrm{val}}$ so~(OV) is not lost as $\eta\to 0$.
Soft-on-all-$[0,1]$ is stable but \textbf{not} a (iii) path for score-threshold $\mathcal{C}_\theta$.
\end{remark}
