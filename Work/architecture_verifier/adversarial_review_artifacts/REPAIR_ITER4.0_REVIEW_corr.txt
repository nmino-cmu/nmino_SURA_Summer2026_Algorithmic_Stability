No. ART-04c does not yet provide a deterministic, non-forgeable CERTIFIED path.

### CRITICAL

1. **CERTIFY is not bound to the reviewed proof or an identifiable certifier act.** `PROOF_CERTIFY.target_digest` binds only the Claim. `ProofEvidence` records a proposer and decision digest, but no reviewed `proof_evidence_digest`, certifier principal, `PROOF_CERTIFIER` binding, review outcome, or certification event. “Certifying path” is therefore not computable. One Claim-level approval can certify a substituted proof, while implementations may disagree whether the human gate operator is also the certifier. [I-CERTIFY-01](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/04-agents/IDENTITY_AUTHORITY_INDEPENDENCE.md:127), [ProofEvidence](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/07-schemas/CANONICAL_OBJECTS.md:454), [gate target](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/15-human-gates/HUMAN_GATES.md:50)

   Required repair: introduce an immutable certification record binding the Claim, exact ProofEvidence/attachment, proposer binding epoch, certifier binding epoch, outcome, and event sequence; require a live `PROOF_CERTIFIER` binding. The HD must target that certification record, not merely the Claim.

2. **The authority graph is self-enrollable.** Principal registration, role binding, provenance registration, and human-decision recording are merely routed through Commit; no rule authorizes who may perform those transitions. `I.Commit.Command` contains no authenticated caller, while the `COMMITTER` role is never enforced. A caller can therefore register its own HUMAN Principal, grant itself `HUMAN_GATE_OPERATOR`, and commit its signed approval. Serialization is not authorization. [interfaces](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/04-agents/IDENTITY_AUTHORITY_INDEPENDENCE.md:151), [RoleBinding roles](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/04-agents/IDENTITY_AUTHORITY_INDEPENDENCE.md:55), [Commit command](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/06-state/MUTATION_AND_AUTHORITATIVE_STATE.md:74), [Commit pipeline](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/06-state/MUTATION_AND_AUTHORITATIVE_STATE.md:105)

   Required repair: define product-neutral genesis/trust roots and an authorization matrix for every identity transition; bind each command to an authenticated caller and require the appropriate live binding.

3. **IndependenceDomain is caller-selectable rather than authoritative.** Nothing requires a RoleBinding’s principal to belong to its referenced domain; domains have no registration/authorization/lifecycle rule; overlapping domain facts are not closed transitively; and multiple concurrent bindings can select different domains. Even one principal can reference two disjoint domains that omit itself and pass the set-intersection test. [RoleBinding domain](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/04-agents/IDENTITY_AUTHORITY_INDEPENDENCE.md:58), [IndependenceDomain](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/04-agents/IDENTITY_AUTHORITY_INDEPENDENCE.md:77)

   Required repair: derive one authoritative independence closure for each principal, policy, and event; require self-membership; union overlapping memberships into equivalence components; reject ambiguous concurrent domain epochs.

4. **HumanDecision hashing is circular.** `decision_digest = H(all normative fields)` includes `signature_material`, while that signature must verify over `decision_digest`. This requires a signature over a digest containing the signature itself and has no generally constructible or deterministic meaning. [HumanDecision schema and validation](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/04-agents/IDENTITY_AUTHORITY_INDEPENDENCE.md:94)

   Required repair: hash an unsigned canonical decision payload, sign that payload digest, and optionally hash the resulting signed envelope separately.

### HIGH

1. **Principal and model identities are not canonically bound.** `principal_digest` omits `kind`, allowing HUMAN, SERVICE, and MODEL_RUNTIME records to share a digest; status/revocation is mixed into the record without a monotone lifecycle rule. Model provenance is self-declared, uses the context-dependent phrase “when available,” and is not attested or bound to the exact proposal/certification act. Consequently `kind=HUMAN` and model-swap checks can differ across implementations. [Principal](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/04-agents/IDENTITY_AUTHORITY_INDEPENDENCE.md:16), [ModelProvenance](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/04-agents/IDENTITY_AUTHORITY_INDEPENDENCE.md:35)

   Required repair: include immutable `kind` and issuer policy in the identity digest; maintain revocation in a separate monotone event-indexed status table; require a schema-defined provenance attestation and bind its exact digest/binding epoch to every credited act.

2. **The governing floor and temporal predicates conflict.** ART-07b still computes CERTIFIED through the revoked `I-HD-01`, while ART-04c requires `I-CERTIFY-01`. HD validity depends on an unspecified “live” binding and use-time event, but `DerivedProofFloor` has no state/event parameter. ART-15 supplies no per-gate expiry requirements despite ART-05 requiring expiring Human Decisions. ART-06b also defers proof-floor transitions while ART-04c says certification attachment is allowed, without formally distinguishing derived evidence changes from axis writes. Strict and permissive validators can therefore disagree on the same state. [floor equation](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/07-schemas/CANONICAL_OBJECTS.md:413), [revocation pointer](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/07-schemas/CANONICAL_OBJECTS.md:447), [HD time checks](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/04-agents/IDENTITY_AUTHORITY_INDEPENDENCE.md:113), [proof-floor deferral](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/06-state/MUTATION_AND_AUTHORITATIVE_STATE.md:148), [expiry requirement](/Users/nicholasmino/Desktop/Research/Work/architecture/05-authority/AUTHORITY_MATRIX.md:27)

   Required repair: replace the old floor equation, define certification validity at an exact state head/event, specify which bindings are checked at proposal, decision, attachment, and use times, and explicitly allow evidence attachment while prohibiting caller-authored floor-axis writes.
